  Hello. I'm AmateurPro, the author of the STUG Custom Cursors folder you'recurrently accessing. After two
 years of being involved with STUG, I've always wanted to do something to contribute to this game.
 Therefore, I've created a custom cursor pack for STUG lovers (including myself lol). These cursors are
 partly my own ideas and designs, and the rest I sourced directly from STUG.io on Google Chrome. the
 animated emotes aren't available as GIFs; they only appear frame by frame. Therefore, I had to use
 Snipping Tool and remove the background before editing them in CapCut. Sounds complicated, right? (yep)
 Also, emotes like Tabby and Salty have animations but don't exist as frames, and I couldn't find their
 GIFs, so I couldn't edit and create animated cursors for them. Also thanks to Adobe Illustrator, I was
 able to design these custom cursors that I came up with.
 Anyway, I really hope this cursor pack makes your desktop a little more fun :)
