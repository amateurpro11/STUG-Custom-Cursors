 ***STEPS TO CHANGE YOUR CURSORS***
 Step 1: Press Win + R on your keyboard

 Step 2: If you see a small window in the bottom left corner, type main.cpl and press Enter

 Step 3: Choose Pointers

 Step 4: Now, click the Browse button, then navigate to the folder containing the cursors you want to change.

 Step 5: Select the correct cursor for each role, then click apply to make the changes.

  Done! Now you have your favorite custom cursors!
